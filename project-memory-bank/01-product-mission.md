# 01 · Product Mission

## Mission statement

**Be the trustworthy daily execution layer that turns intention into shipped capability, with AI as a reliable collaborator — not a black box.**

---

## What this means concretely

The product earns its place in a user's day when:

- They open it **first thing** to know what to do.
- They keep it open **while they work** because it tracks them, not the other way around.
- They close their day **inside it** — reflecting, capturing artifacts, updating the plan.
- A week later, the AI remembers them — what they tried, what stuck, what to revisit.

If the product fails any of those four moments, retention collapses and the mission isn't being executed.

---

## Daily-life jobs the product does

1. **"Tell me what to work on today and why."** — execution clarity grounded in the user's roadmap, not generic advice.
2. **"Help me think through this without losing context."** — mentor conversations that have memory.
3. **"Track what I actually did, not what I claimed."** — passive sensing via the VSCode layer + active capture in the desktop.
4. **"Turn my work into something I can show."** — artifact generation: project pages, portfolio entries, narrative.
5. **"Tell me what I'm getting better at."** — capability intelligence over time, not vibes.

Each of these maps to one of the seven engines (see `10-system-architecture.md`).

---

## Mission constraints

These are not negotiable; if a feature breaks one of these, the feature is wrong:

- **Trust > flash.** Users must be able to predict what the system will do.
- **User owns data.** Local-first, exportable, deletable.
- **AI is a collaborator, not an oracle.** Explainability and user agency over slick autonomy.
- **Offline never blocks core workflows.** Mentor, planner, tracker all work with no network.
- **Workflows compound.** Today's data must improve next month's mentorship.

---

## Anti-mission

We are explicitly NOT trying to:

- Maximize "session time" with engagement tricks.
- Replace the user's judgment (we augment it).
- Replace the IDE, terminal, or browser.
- Make the user dependent on a single AI vendor.
- Make the product unusable when the cloud is unavailable.
